About Me

Full-stack development and AI.

I'm Stef, a full stack software engineer with a background in computer systems and AI engineering. I earned my MSc in Computing Science at the University of Groningen in August 2026, following a BSc in Computer Science at Oregon State University in June 2024.

What I've been working on

At Ocean Recap, I've built and shipped features for a maritime SaaS platform using Vue.js and Symfony/PHP, backed by integration and end-to-end tests. I led development of an XML-driven document configuration system supporting over 100 templates, and document-template versioning that preserves compatibility with existing client contracts.

My master's thesis explored microscopy modality translation using conditional generative adversarial networks. As a graduate research intern at the University of Groningen's PRISMA Lab, I used PyTorch and high-performance computing infrastructure to evaluate image-to-image translation methods supporting microscopy segmentation and tracking.
